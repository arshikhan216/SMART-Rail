============================================================
1. PROBLEM CONTEXT
============================================================

Indian Railways has maintenance activities across multiple departments:

1. Engineering
2. Signal & Telecommunication (S&T)
3. Traction Distribution

Maintenance information is maintained independently in systems conceptually represented as:

- TMS → Track/Engineering maintenance and defects
- SMMS → Signalling maintenance
- TDMS → Traction Distribution maintenance
- COA → Corridor/block availability
- Train timetable and goods/freight train forecast

The current process is decentralized and manual.

This causes:

- Poor coordination between departments
- Inefficient use of maintenance blocks
- Duplicate infrastructure downtime
- Poor utilization of available block windows
- Maintenance delays
- Increased asset downtime
- Potential train disruption
- Reduced infrastructure availability

The proposed system must integrate these data sources and automatically generate optimized maintenance block plans.

4. Resource capacity cannot be exceeded.

5. Conflicting train movements must be respected.

6. Department/resource constraints must be respected.

7. Maintenance deadlines should be respected.

8. Safety constraints are HARD constraints.

9. Critical maintenance should receive strong priority.

10. Same-block coordination should receive positive benefit.
